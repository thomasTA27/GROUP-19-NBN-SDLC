# white-paper/

The white paper itself: the main deliverable of this project, defining
how developers work when continuously using LLMs across the software
development lifecycle.

## What belongs here

- `modules/` — one file per SDLC stage, following `modules/TEMPLATE.md`
- `governance/` — oversight, approval, and AI-use policy content that
  applies across stages
- `learning-paths/` — onboarding material for teams adopting the
  methodology
- `storyboard/` — the narrative flow/structure of the white paper as a
  presentable artifact

## What does not belong here

- Raw research (source notes, articles, findings) — that goes in
  [research/](../research/), and gets cited from here once synthesized
- Lifecycle map source or exports — that goes in [design/](../design/)
- Meeting notes or sprint records — that goes in [project/](../project/)
