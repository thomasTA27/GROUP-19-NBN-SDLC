## What a pilot is for

A dry run of the session with a teammate standing in for the real participant.

The pilot does **not** test the modules. It tests the machinery around them: does
the script make sense to read out, do the timings hold, does the questionnaire
arrive at the right moment, does anything break.

That is why a teammate works as the stand-in even though they already know the
modules. Knowing the content would ruin a real session, but here it does not
matter, because we are watching the clock and the script rather than their
understanding.

## How to run it

Run both scripts, back to back if possible.

Follow the script exactly, including the "we are testing the module, not you"
opening and the "what do you think it means?" rule. The point is to find out
whether those lines work when said out loud.

Keep the clock running and write down what time each phase actually ends, not
what time the script says it should.

Do not fix anything during the pilot. Note it and carry on.
