# Module usability questionnaire — facilitator scoring

Use with [the shared response form](module-usability-questionnaire.md). Keep this scoring sheet out of view while the participant responds. Version 1, 2026-10-11.

## Administration

1. Make a fresh copy of the response form for each participant. Save completed responses outside the participant repositories, in the phase's facilitator-records folder.
2. Give the form immediately after task work and the post-task confidence question, before the debrief: planning minutes 55–60; implementation minutes 45–50.
3. Keep wording, order and response scale identical in both sessions. Do not explain away negative statements or suggest ratings. Refer questions to the written instructions and record any confusion for the debrief.
4. Check that all ten responses are integers from 1 to 5. Ask the participant to resolve blanks or duplicate selections without suggesting an answer. If a response remains missing or invalid, retain the evidence and report the score as unavailable; do not fill it in yourself.

The form follows Brooke's midpoint instruction for participants who feel unable to answer an item. An unanswered item is not automatically a midpoint response.

## Reporting

Report the raw responses alongside the score and observed findings. The score is not a percentage and is not the six-criterion task-success result or the separate before/after confidence rating.

Following the team's plans, use this adapted form as an internal baseline without a published-average comparison, percentile, letter grade or pass threshold. Both sessions use identical items, but different participants, durations and module coverage limit direct comparisons. A single participant per phase does not establish general usability or how common a problem is. Do not interpret individual items as validated standalone measures.

Suggested result sentence:

> Participant [ID] rated [module/version] [score]/100 on our module-adapted SUS questionnaire. Task completion was [result]. The main observed difficulties were [findings]. This was one participant's session with [coverage]; the score is an internal baseline.
