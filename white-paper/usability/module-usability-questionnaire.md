## Instructions

Rate the module you used in this session. Here, “module” means the written guidance, rather than Claude Code, VS Code, Teams or the example application. There are no right or wrong answers; we are evaluating the guidance, not your ability.

Choose one number for each statement based on your immediate impression. If you feel unable to answer a statement, choose the midpoint, 3. Record your chosen number in the Response column.

| Response | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Strongly disagree |
| 2 | Disagree |
| 3 | Neither agree nor disagree |
| 4 | Agree |
| 5 | Strongly agree |

## Attribution
