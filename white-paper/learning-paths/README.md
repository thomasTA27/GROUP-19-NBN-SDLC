# white-paper/learning-paths/

Onboarding material for teams adopting the methodology described in the
white paper — how a developer, or a team, ramps up on working with LLMs
across the SDLC.

## What belongs here

- Sequenced onboarding guides (e.g. "week one," role-based paths)
- Prerequisite knowledge and recommended reading order through the
  `modules/` content

## What does not belong here

- The stage modules themselves — those live in [../modules/](../modules/)
  and are referenced from here, not duplicated
- Raw research or training material sourced externally — that goes in
  [research/](../../research/)
