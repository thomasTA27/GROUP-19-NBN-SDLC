# design/validation/

Evidence that design decisions in the lifecycle map (stage definitions,
flow, scope) were reviewed or tested with real input, not just assumed.

## What belongs here

- Notes or summaries from stakeholder review sessions
- Feedback captured on the lifecycle map and how it was resolved

## What does not belong here

- The lifecycle map exports themselves — those go in
  [../lifecycle-map/](../lifecycle-map/)
- General research — that goes in [research/](../../research/)
