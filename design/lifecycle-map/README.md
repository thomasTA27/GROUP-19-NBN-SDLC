## What belongs here

- Snapshot exports of the agreed map (PNG/PDF) at key milestones, dated in
  the filename (e.g. `lifecycle-map-2026-W2.pdf`)
- `proposals/` — everyone's own draft diagram, made before the team
  agrees on the single map above

## What does not belong here

- Any attempt to recreate or edit the agreed map outside Miro — always
  update the live board and re-export
- Personal draft proposals — those go in [proposals/](proposals/), not
  mixed in with the agreed map's exports
