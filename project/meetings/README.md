# project/meetings/

Meeting notes and minutes for the team and with the client/supervisor.

## What belongs here

- Dated meeting notes (e.g. `2026-08-23-client-checkin.md`)
- Action items and decisions made in each meeting

## What does not belong here

- Sprint plans/reviews — those go in [../sprints/](../sprints/)
- Deliverable content discussed in a meeting — that lives in its own
  top-level folder and gets linked from the note, not copied into it
