# research/benchmarking/

Comparative data on AI tools and models for development tasks — how they
perform against each other and against non-AI baselines.

## What belongs here

- Captures (using [../TEMPLATE.md](../TEMPLATE.md)) on benchmark results,
  tool comparisons, and evaluation methodology

## What does not belong here

- Narrative research on AI use in a specific stage — that goes in
  [../ai-in-development/](../ai-in-development/) or
  [../ai-in-testing/](../ai-in-testing/)
- Synthesized conclusions — those go in [../synthesis.md](../synthesis.md)
