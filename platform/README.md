# platform/

The demonstration platform that shows the AI-integrated SDLC methodology
in practice — a Next.js site, built in Sprint 2.

## Status: not started

This folder is a placeholder until Sprint 2. Scoping happens in Sprint 1;
the site itself is scaffolded and built afterward.

**This is the one exception to "no code" in this repository.** Once the
Next.js site exists, its `package.json`, build config, and application
code live here — nowhere else in the repo. Until then, do not add any of
that here yet.

## What will belong here (from Sprint 2)

- The Next.js application demonstrating the methodology defined in
  [white-paper/](../white-paper/)
- Notes on how the platform maps back to specific white paper modules
- The deployed URL, once it exists (see the root [README.md](../README.md)
  for the placeholder)

## What does not belong here

- The methodology definition itself — that lives in
  [white-paper/](../white-paper/)
- Research, design, or project-management content — those have their own
  top-level folders
